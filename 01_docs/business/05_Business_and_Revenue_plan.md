# Business and Revenue Plan

Initial Phase

1. Deploy the Project to the Restaurant to be used for till the end of year
**Why**
- It enables **real-world validation** of the product using actual users instead of assumptions.
- It helps identify **operational bottlenecks** in ordering, fulfillment, and delivery workflows.
- It allows collection of **usage data** to inform product improvements and future analytics features.
- It lowers adoption friction for the restaurant, increasing the chances of **successful onboarding and retention**.
- It provides an opportunity to **iterate quickly** and fix critical bugs before introducing paid usage.
- It builds **trust and dependency**, making the restaurant more willing to pay later.
- It gives you a **strong case study** and portfolio project with real impact and metrics.

After Initial Phase

1. **Commission per Order (1%)** is a revenue model where the platform charges the restaurant **1% of the total order value** for every order processed through the app.
- For example, if a student places a 50 GHS order, the platform earns **0.50 GHS** from that transaction.
- This applies **regardless of payment method**, including cash-on-delivery, because the commission is calculated based on the **order total**, not the payment type.
- Commissions can be invoiced weekly or monthly, providing a steady revenue stream while restaurants benefit from order management, analytics, and visibility on the app.
