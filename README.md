# Digital Ordering System - Project Plan

## Project Title

Restaurant Mobile Ordering App

## Objective

Develop a mobile application that lets students browse the campus restaurant menu and place orders directly from their phones. Customers can pay securely via mobile money or choose cash-on-delivery for convenience. The restaurant admin can view incoming orders, update order status, and manage fulfillment through a desktop dashboard. The platform ensures fast, seamless food delivery on campus while providing analytics and order tracking for the restaurant.

## Problem Statement

The current ordering process relies on phone calls, which often leads to miscommunications and delays. There is no way to track orders in real time, making it difficult for both customers and the restaurant. This results in slower service and a less convenient experience for students. Our app streamlines the process, enabling accurate orders, real-time tracking, and faster, more reliable food delivery on campus.
## Solution

A mobile ordering system that allows users to browse the restaurant menu and place orders directly from their phones. Customers can select their preferred meals, customize orders, and choose their payment method. Payments can be completed digitally through mobile money or via cash-on-delivery. The system streamlines the ordering process, reducing wait times and improving the overall customer experience.
